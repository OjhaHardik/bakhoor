About Us

Bakhoor Al Barkaah started with a simple idea: the scent that filled our grandparents' homes shouldn't be hard to find. We blend and pack every batch ourselves, in small runs, so what reaches your door is close to what came off the coal in our own workshop.

Our Story

Bakhoor has moved between homes for centuries — carried back from Hajj, handed over at a doorway, kept burning during Jumu'ah. We wanted a version of that tradition that was easy to order and just as easy to trust, without losing what made it worth keeping in the first place.

What Goes Into Each Batch

We source aloeswood, resins, and oils from small suppliers who've worked with these materials for years, then blend and hand-pack each order in small batches rather than holding large stock. Nothing sits on a shelf for long before it reaches you.

Our Promise

  • Ingredients named plainly, with nothing hidden in the blend.
  • Small-batch production, so freshness isn't an afterthought.
  • A team that actually reads and answers what you write to us.
