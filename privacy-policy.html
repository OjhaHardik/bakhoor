Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This explains what we collect when you use this site or place an order, why we collect it, and what we do — and don't do — with it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as:

  • Your name, email, phone number, and shipping address when you check out or create an account.
  • Order details — what you bought, when, and for how much.
  • Anything you send us through the Contact Us page.

We don't collect or store your card or payment details — that's handled entirely by our payment gateway.

2. How We Use It

  • To process and deliver your order, and send updates about it.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To keep basic records for accounting and legal purposes.
  • To improve the site — what pages get used, what doesn't.

We don't sell your information to anyone.

3. Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies to keep you signed in and remember your cart. Details are in our separate Cookie Policy.

4. Sharing With Others

We share order information with the people who need it to fulfil your order — our payment gateway, and the courier delivering your package. We don't hand your data to anyone beyond that without telling you, except where required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, but no method of storage or transmission online is completely risk-free. If you notice anything that looks wrong with your account, let us know.

6. Your Rights

You can ask us to show you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, subject to what we're legally required to keep (like tax records). Email us and we'll sort it out.

7. Children's Privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

Questions about your data? Reach us through our Contact Us page.
