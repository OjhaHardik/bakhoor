Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Cookies are small text files a site stores in your browser. Here's what we use them for on this site.

1. What Cookies Do Here

We use cookies to keep you signed in between visits, remember what's in your cart, and understand which pages get visited so we can improve the site. We don't use cookies to build an advertising profile of you.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — keep you logged in and your cart intact as you move between pages. The site won't work properly without these.
  • Preference cookies — remember small choices you've made, like a previously entered shipping address.
  • Analytics cookies — tell us which pages get used, in aggregate, so we know what's working.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking essential cookies will likely break sign-in and checkout on this site, so we'd suggest only clearing them if something looks stuck.

4. Changes to This Policy

If how we use cookies changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

5. Contact Us

Questions about cookies on this site? Reach us through our Contact Us page.
